# MAIHDA: Multilevel Analysis of Individual Heterogeneity and Discriminatory Accuracy

Provides a comprehensive toolkit for conducting Multilevel Analysis of
Individual Heterogeneity and Discriminatory Accuracy (MAIHDA). Methods
are described in Merlo (2018)
[doi:10.1016/j.socscimed.2017.12.018](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.socscimed.2017.12.018)
and Evans et al. (2018)
[doi:10.1016/j.socscimed.2017.11.011](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.socscimed.2017.11.011)
. Automatically generates intersectional strata, fits analytical models,
extracts statistics, and produces visualizations.
